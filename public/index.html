What's new

More than overlays. Your stream, on autopilot.

Mint keeps growing. These are the newest ways to make your live feel alive, without extra apps or extra work mid-stream.

New

Actions & Events

Tell Mint what should happen when something happens, then forget about it. A chat command can save a clip. A Rose can play a sound and switch your scene. A raid can thank the crowd out loud.

  • Triggers: chat commands, gifts, new followers, shares, fan club level-ups, and Twitch cheers, subs, gift subs and raids.
  • Steps: press a keyboard shortcut, show a text alert, play a video or sound, change your voice, read a message aloud, switch your OBS scene, or send a web request.
  • Under your control: choose who can use each command, set cooldowns, and delay any step so things happen in order.
Beta

Live Party

Your viewers become dancers in a 3D club that lives on your stream. Everyone who joins steps onto the floor, big gifts take the main stage, and the room fills with energy until it explodes into a celebration.

  • Ten dance rooms such as Neon Grove, Latin, Country, Hip-Hop, EDM, R&B and Rock, plus holiday themes.
  • Gifts have impact: wings, headliner moments and a room-energy meter your chat fills together.
  • Easy to run: a simple control room, with the stage added to TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS as one link.
New

Word Garden

A word game your chat plays together, right on your stream. A keyword appears, viewers type what comes to mind, and every answer blooms on screen with the name and avatar of whoever found it first.

  • 1,000 ready-made prompts with many ways to spell each answer, so guesses count.
  • Rounds, timers and a live leaderboard, with a celebration for the round winner and the top bloomer.
  • Built for phone screens: a tall layout that fits a vertical live, with your own round length.
New

Countdown Timer

A clock on your stream that your viewers keep alive. Every gift coin, tap, comment, share, subscription and follow adds time, and you decide what each one is worth.

  • You set the rules: even 0.2 seconds per coin. Start it at any time you type, like 90 minutes or 1 hour 45.
  • Everyone sees who helped: pop-ups show who added time, with their profile picture and what they did.
  • It is an on-screen clock. It shows how long you have promised to stay on; it does not keep your live open by itself, so you end the live when it reaches zero.
Standout

Overlay Studio

Combine any widgets into one scene: leaderboards, goals, alerts, gift scroll, videos. Place them where you want and copy a single link into TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS. Change a font or move a box and your stream updates on its own.

  • One link instead of a dozen browser sources to add and keep in sync.
  • Vertical and horizontal: save a layout for each, and make a video or alert play only on the shape it was designed for.
  • Transparent by design. The guide frame helps you place things; your stream shows only your widgets.

How it works

Live in about five minutes.

You do not need to be technical. If you can copy and paste a link, you can use Mint Overlays.

Install and connect

Download the app, type your channel name on the Connections page and press Connect. There is no TikTok login or password to enter.

Copy a link

Pick a widget, or build a scene in Overlay Studio, and copy its link. Paste it into TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS as a browser source.

Go live

Gifts, follows, chat and likes now drive your overlays, sounds and actions. A built-in guide and page tours help the first time through.

Questions

Good to know before you start.

Do I need OBS?

No. Every widget gives you a link you can add as a browser source in TikTok LIVE Studio, and the same link works in OBS if you use it. OBS is only needed for the optional scene-switching step in Actions & Events.

Which platforms does it work with?

You can connect TikTok, Twitch, Kick and YouTube. Mint is built around TikTok LIVE, so gifts, battles and likes are TikTok features. Twitch adds cheers, subscriptions, gift subs and raids as triggers.

Do I have to give you my TikTok login?

No. You connect by typing your channel name. Mint Overlays never asks for your TikTok password.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet. Mint Overlays is a Windows 10 and 11 app.
